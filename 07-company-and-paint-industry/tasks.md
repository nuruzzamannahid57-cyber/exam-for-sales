# 07 · Company & industry tasks (answers in `solutions.md`)

## 🟢 Easy (10 XP each)
1. What was the company's former name?  2. Which Japanese group is it part of?  3. When does its financial year end?
4. Order the painting layers: Emulsion, Putty, Primer.

## 🟡 Medium (20 XP each)
5. Visit nerolac.com and list 5 product *categories* (not brands).  6. Name two competitors and one thing each is known for (from a news article).
7. Explain "tinting machine" to a dealer in 2 sentences.

## 🔴 Hard (40 XP each)
8. Make a one-page **company fact sheet** with 10 facts, each with its source link.
9. Why would a paint company use a dealer network + incentive schemes? List 4 benefits and 2 risks.
10. Imagine you manage dealer claims: list 5 checks before approving a scheme claim.

## 🏆 Boss (100 XP)
