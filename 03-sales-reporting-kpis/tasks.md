# 03 · KPI tasks (answers in `solutions.md`)

## 🟢 Easy (10 XP each)
1. Target 5,000,000; actual 4,300,000. Achievement %?
2. Last year 3,800,000; this year 4,500,000. Growth % (2 decimals)?
3. Region sold 1.2M of a company total 6M. Contribution %?
4. 920 units delivered of 1,000 ordered. Fill rate?

## 🟡 Medium (20 XP each)
5. Day 15 of a 30-day month: sold 2.4M, target 6M. Current run rate per day, projected month-end, and achievement of target at projection?
6. Same case: what daily run rate is *required* for the remaining 15 days to hit target?
7. Collected 8.5M of 10M due. Collection efficiency? How much is still pending?
8. 40 orders worth 6,000,000 total. Average order value?

## 🔴 Hard (40 XP each)
9. Using `data/sales_orders.csv` + `data/targets.csv` (from topic 01): compute each region's achievement % and rank them. Which 2 regions need attention?
10. Pareto: how many dealers make up ~80% of total revenue? (use pivot + cumulative %)
11. Month-over-month growth for each month Feb–Jun. Which month dropped most?

## 🏆 Boss (100 XP)
Write a **half-page weekly sales report** (headline, 5 KPIs, 3 risks, 3 actions) from your topic 01 dashboard data, as if for your sales manager.
