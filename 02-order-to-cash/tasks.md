# 02 · Order-to-Cash tasks  (answers in `solutions.md`)

## 🟢 Easy (10 XP each)
1. Put in order: Invoice, Sales Order, Payment, Delivery, Credit check.
2. Which document proves the dealer actually received the goods?
3. A dealer returns damaged goods. Which document do you issue — credit note or debit note?
4. Define "credit limit" in one sentence.
5. Invoice date 10 March, credit terms 30 days. Due date?

## 🟡 Medium (20 XP each)
6. Dealer orders 50 units @ BDT 1,400, trade discount 8%, VAT 15% on the discounted value. Final invoice total?
7. Dealer limit 500,000; outstanding 380,000; new order value 150,000. Release or hold? By how much is the limit exceeded?
8. Invoice dated 12 Jan 2026, 30-day terms. Today is 20 Mar 2026. How many days overdue? Which aging bucket (0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+) counts from the *due date*?
9. A dealer says "you charged me 1,450, the agreed price is 1,400". The invoice qty is 80. How large is the credit note (before VAT)?

## 🔴 Hard (40 XP each)
10. Dealer returns 10 units billed at 1,400 with 8% discount. Calculate the credit note including 15% VAT.
11. Dealer statement shows: Inv A 120,000 (due 5 Mar), Inv B 80,000 (due 25 Mar), payment received 150,000 (unallocated). On 30 Mar, using oldest-first allocation, what is outstanding per invoice and the total overdue?
12. Explain, in 5 lines, what you do when a dealer asks you to release an order while over credit limit.

## 🏆 Boss (100 XP)
Write a one-page **SOP** (standard operating procedure) for "Order processing with credit check", with: steps, who approves, documents created, and 3 common errors + how to avoid them.
