# 09 · Interview + Mock Exam 🏁 (final boss)

## STAR answers
**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult (with numbers). Prepare 5 stories:
1. Handling many accounts at once · 2. Fixing a data/reporting problem · 3. A conflict or escalation you resolved
4. A tool/dashboard/process you built · 5. A mistake and what you changed afterwards

> Tip: your key-account and dashboard experience maps directly onto Sales Admin: follow-ups, reporting, accuracy, dealer/merchant communication. Use *your* real numbers (don't invent).

## Likely questions
- Tell me about yourself (60 sec: present → past → why this role).
- What does a Sales Admin do in one paragraph?
- How do you prioritise 10 urgent tasks? · How do you handle a dealer who is angry about a delay?
- What would you do if you found a pricing error after dispatch?
- Excel: which functions do you use daily?
- Why this company? (see topic 07)
