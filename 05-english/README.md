# 05 · Business English ✍️

## 1) Email skeleton (works every time)
**Subject:** specific and short → *Payment reminder – Invoice INV-2045*
**Greeting** · **Purpose in line 1** · **Details** (dates, numbers) · **Clear action + deadline** · **Polite close**

## 2) Phrases
- Opening: "I am writing to confirm…", "Following up on our call…"
- Request: "Could you please share… by Thursday?"
- Delay/problem: "Unfortunately, the dispatch is delayed due to… We expect delivery by…"
- Close: "Please let me know if you need anything else.", "Best regards,"

## 3) Sales-admin vocabulary
invoice · outstanding · reconcile · discrepancy · dispatch · backorder · rebate · claim · credit note · lead time · escalate · settle · remittance · variance · allocation
