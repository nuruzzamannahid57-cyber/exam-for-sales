# 04 · Quantitative & Analytical Aptitude 🧠

**Rule of the game:** speed + accuracy. Do every task with a timer. Aim: 1 minute per question.

## Cheat sheet
- x% of N = N × x/100 · % change = (new − old)/old × 100
- Increase by a% then decrease by a% → net loss (a²/100)%
- Successive discounts d1, d2: final = P × (1−d1)(1−d2) (not d1+d2!)
- Profit % = (SP − CP)/CP × 100 · SP = CP × (1 + profit%)
- Ratio a:b of total T → a/(a+b)×T
- Time & work: rate = 1/days; together = sum of rates
- Compound growth: P(1+r)^n
- Series: check differences, ratios, squares, primes, alternating patterns
